Why Protein First?

Protein is the most satiating macronutrient. It builds and repairs muscle, keeps you fuller for longer, and has a higher thermic effect than carbs or fat — meaning your body burns more calories just digesting it.

  • Muscle synthesis — 1.6–2.2g per kg of bodyweight is the evidence-based target.
  • Satiety — high-protein diets reduce hunger hormones like ghrelin.
  • Body recomposition — retain muscle in a calorie deficit when protein is high.
